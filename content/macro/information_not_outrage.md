---
title: I want to be informed not outraged
date: 2026-10-13
---

The rage will come as it should, this is not to pacify.

But ive been sitting in rage for the world for what feels like a decade. I let outrage become my driving force. Its angry bursts of hard work fueled by the fire of an article about an evil company. They never stop coming, im white hot, combusting, how could I ever burn out? I cant help but step forward and be evermore enraged at those who didnt just feel that push. They always tell you this mentality is not sustainable along the way but they must just not feel the rage that I feel, it feels like it will last forever. 

And it could, if I let it. But the result wont be a solution and life well lived, and the people you are mad for are no better off.

You have to let the rage flow through, you cant keep it, hoarding it for virtue.
You are not guilty of negligence if you let it seep back out through your fingertips. 

Find a way to step forward without flames at your back and you might get where you actually want to go.
