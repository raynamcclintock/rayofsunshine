---
title: I could doom us but not because it will launch the nukes
date: 2026-10-02
---

This doomerist, inevitable AI apocalypse conversation that wont stop invading my newsfeed instead of the other doomery options like the war we are waging in Iran and the ICE shootings in the streets and the carbon we keep pumping into the atmosphere is a bad idea.

I dont even know enough about this to know if AI could launch the nukes and how well they can hack into everything ever. Cybersecurity hasn't tickled my brain.

But decisions are the highest form of power. So why would we possible think that politicians would ever voluntarily give up power to a robot? Why is there this idea that AI will be governing us all and making every decision in the future. That is something so fundamentally human and ingrained in our current society that we can actually take a collective sigh of relief. Who pursues power and why and the concept that absolute power corrupts absolutely is a problem for another day (or for humans to continue philosophizing about and wresting with for the rest of time).

The guy in the suit who bought and sold and compromised himself to be the guy with his finger over the button... yeah he's not gonna give that up to a clinker.


The doom comes from the culture. The concept of helplessness or that we can pawn off all problem solving to some model (that was trained on reddit posts and AI generated data by losers with no light in their eyes who would rather live in a video game). Why would this model be ultimately right and moral with such parents? The doom comes from the 25 news stories we wake up to every day that say there is a 10% chance AI will kill us all. That there wont be a single job left in a few years. Yes its all coming from the CEOs mouth so it seems pretty real, what a unique marketing strategy.

Its the perceived loss of agency that will doom us all.
